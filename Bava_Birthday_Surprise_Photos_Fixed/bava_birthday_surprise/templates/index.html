I don't know next year nenu ekkada untano, ela untano, evaritho untano. Uhinchukuntene chala bayam ga undhi. Nuv untav ane okeokka dhiryam nannu nadipisthundhi.

Naku oka bayam undhi — ekkada nenu change aipothano ani. Kaani nuv na life lo unnantha varaku nenu change avvanu ani believe chesthunna.

E madhya work valla sarigga matladalekapothunna. Okappudu ammo matladakapothe malli gap osthadhi ani oka chinna fear undedhi. Ippudu naaku alanti fear em ledhu, endhukante nuv em jarigina naathone untav ani nammakam naalo strong ga kaligindhi. Andhuke e madhya brathimaldatam lantivi em cheyyatledhu.

I am always positive when you are with me. And matladalekapothunnandhuku sorry — but I am always and every time with you.

Nuv inka nee career vishyam lo serious ga alochinchali. Idhe udyogam, idhe jeetham tho manam mundhuki vellalem. Alternatives vethakatam start cheyyali. I hope neeku inka manchi opportunity ochi, nee appulu anni theeripoyi, nuv happy ga undali.

Edhi emaina, nenu ninnu vedhinchadam maathram maananu. I don't let you miss me. 😘

Once again, happy birthday Bava. Love you so much, my dear husbanduuu. ❤️
